Build Your AI Boundaries

Most engineers don't set AI boundaries — they just react. This 5-minute tool helps you define exactly where AI stops and you begin. Get a shareable Boundary Score.

How it works

Step 1 of 5

Boundary #1 — Usage Pattern

How often do you reach for AI while coding?

Think about a typical workday over the past two weeks.

Boundary #2 — Ownership

When you ship AI-assisted code, how much of it do you deeply understand?

Be honest — this is about knowing, not about what you tell others.

Boundary #3 — Protected Time

Do you have recurring time blocks where you code without AI?

This could be mornings, certain days, or specific project phases.

Boundary #4 — Discomfort Signal

When AI suggests something that feels wrong, what happens?

Think about the last time you sensed something was off in AI-generated code.

Boundary #5 — Skill Maintenance

How often do you deliberately practice a skill without AI assistance?

This could be debugging, algorithm design, writing tests, or any craft skill.
