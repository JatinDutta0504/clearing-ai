How to Send — Manual via Mail

1

Collect subscriber emails — watch for Formspree submissions at formspree.io/forms/YOUR_FORM_ID/manage

Formspree submission → subscriber list → manual send queue

2

Send Day 1 immediately — use himalaya:

himalaya compose --to subscriber@email.com --subject "The Sunday Night Reckoning" --attach email-course/ai-fatigue-reset-email-1.html

Or open the HTML in browser and copy content as email body

3

Track in the sequence log: Create email-course/subscribers.csv with: email, signup_date, day1_sent through day5_sent

4

Day 2-5: Send every 24h. Open the .html files and copy content into your email client.

⚙️ Formspree Setup — Required Before Launch

All email-course forms currently have YOUR_FORM_ID as placeholder. Sunny needs to configure Formspree to enable real email capture.

  • 1. Sign up at formspree.io (free tier: 50 submissions/month)
  • 2. Create a form → copy your Form ID (e.g., xpwqqvln)
  • 3. Replace YOUR_FORM_ID in: email-course.html, email-course/index.html
  • 4. Configure Formspree email routing to notify when new subscribers sign up
  • 5. Set up Zapier: Formspree → Gmail/himalaya → 5-email sequence with 1-day intervals
