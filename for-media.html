About

What is The Clearing?

The Clearing is an independent research and resource platform built for software engineers navigating AI-accelerated work. Founded in 2025, it covers the cognitive, psychological, and professional dimensions of AI fatigue — from skill atrophy and consultation dependency to recovery frameworks and team-level interventions.

We don't sell courses. We don't take venture capital. We're a small team building the resource we wished existed when we were burned out.

Common Questions

Reporter FAQ

Can I interview someone from The Clearing for my story?
Yes. Fill out the form above or email press@clearing-ai.com. We typically respond within 24 hours and can arrange phone, video, or email interviews. For broadcast outlets, we can provide pre-recorded video statements depending on scheduling.
Can I cite your statistics without an interview?
Absolutely. All statistics on clearing-ai.com are available for citation with credit to "The Clearing Research, 2026." Our methodology is published on each data page. For academic papers or major publications, we recommend confirming exact figures directly with us.
Do you offer embargo access to upcoming data?
Yes. We offer embargoed access to new survey results for journalists under publication deadline. Embargo means you can read and prepare coverage before publication date, but agree not to publish until the embargo lifts. Email press@clearing-ai.com with your deadline.
Can you connect me with an engineer willing to go on the record?
We can. Our community includes engineers from a range of company sizes who have opted in to being contacted for press inquiries. We facilitate the introduction and share their background before doing so. Fill out the form and select "Connection to engineer source.">
Is The Clearing accepting sponsored content or paid placements?
No. The Clearing does not accept sponsored content, paid placements, or advertiser-funded content. We are reader-supported through newsletter subscriptions and occasional grants. Our editorial independence is non-negotiable.
How was the survey conducted?
Our survey is conducted quarterly via The Clearing's newsletter, community channels, and partner developer communities. Sample size varies by quarter (n=1,200–2,500). We publish full methodology alongside results on our Engineer Survey Results page. We do not accept corporate funding for survey design or reporting.
