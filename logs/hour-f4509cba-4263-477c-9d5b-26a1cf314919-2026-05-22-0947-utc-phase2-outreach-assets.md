## AI Brownout — What This Page Is

AI brownout: low-grade, chronic cognitive depletion caused by continuous AI tool use without adequate recovery. Distinct from burnout (clinical exhaustion) and from acute AI fatigue (episodic overwhelm). It's the background hum of "I'm always on, but I'm not really engaged."

This page fills a critical gap in the pillar: we have AI fatigue (acute), burnout (clinical), but nothing specifically on the chronic, sustained, subclinical exhaustion that comes from constant AI-assisted work without recovery.
