**Issue #86: "The Explanation Requirement"**
Theme: Why understanding things too easily means you're not learning. The explanation was the reward for productive struggle — AI made it available instantly, and now the consolidation step is being bypassed.

5 sections:
1. **The Moment You Stopped Learning** — the rubber duck moment vs asking ChatGPT
2. **The Hidden Function of Explanation** — explanation as compression, not communication; Feynman diagnostic
3. **What AI Did to This** — the explanation without the understanding problem; brain registers both the same way
4. **The Specific Skill That's Being Lost** — metacognitive calibration; knowing when you don't understand vs AI telling you code is wrong
5. **The Friday Explanation** — once a week, write in plain language what you understand now that you didn't before; the constraint that surfaces gaps

**Key message:** The goal isn't to avoid AI. It's to notice when you're trading the hard thing (understanding) for the easy thing (explanation) — and to choose deliberately instead of by default.
