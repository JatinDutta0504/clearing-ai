## EM2-2 — Software Design Weekly (Adam)

**To:** adam@softwaredesignweekly.com
**Subject:** The design principle that AI breaks

**Body:**

Hi Adam,

Quick follow-up from my note last week. Not looking to take more of your time — just wanted to share one idea worth considering:

The software design principle AI most reliably breaks is "local cognition" — you should understand the system you built well enough to reason about any part of it from memory, without tooling. Good design meant a developer could hold the whole system in their head.

AI generates systems no single developer can hold in their head anymore. The design constraint has changed. Your readers know this — they feel it in code reviews, architecture discussions, onboarding.

This might be worth a future piece: what does good software design mean when AI writes most of the code?

Happy to write something original if the timing is ever right.

— Sunny
